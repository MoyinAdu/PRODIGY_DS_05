# US Accident Analysis Dashboard (California, 2022-2023)

## Project Overview

In this project, I analyzed traffic accident data from the US Accidents dataset, focusing on California between 2022 and 2023. The goal was to understand accident patterns and identify factors that may contribute to road accidents.

Using Power BI, I explored how location, time, weather, and road features relate to accident occurrences across the state.

## Key Findings

### Location Analysis

* Most accidents were concentrated around major cities and busy highway routes in California.
* Coastal metropolitan areas showed the highest accident density.

### Time Analysis

* Accident frequency increased during peak commuting hours.
* The highest number of accidents occurred:

  * Between 7 AM and 9 AM
  * Between 3 PM and 5 PM

### Weather Analysis

* Most accidents happened during fair weather conditions, which is expected because California experiences fair weather most of the time.
* Cloudy conditions and light rain were also linked to a noticeable number of accidents.

### Road Features Analysis

* Traffic signals and highway junctions were the most common road features found at accident locations.
* These areas may require extra attention because of increased traffic activity and vehicle interactions.

## Tools Used

### Power BI Desktop

* Built the interactive dashboard
* Created maps and visualizations
* Analyzed trends and accident patterns

### Power Query

* Cleaned and prepared the dataset
* Filtered records for California, 2022, and 2023
* Optimized the data model for reporting and analysis

## Conclusion

This analysis helped identify where and when accidents occur most frequently in California. The findings show that location, rush-hour traffic, weather conditions, and road infrastructure all play important roles in accident patterns. These insights can support better road safety planning and decision-making.
